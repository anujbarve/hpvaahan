# Shipping Policy

Kemetyl India Markets Pvt. Ltd. cannot commit to any fixed time for delivery. The delivery of our products will be made between 9:00 am to 6:00 p.m. of the estimated delivery date. It may get delayed on certain days due to extreme weather conditions or governmental regulatory activities.

The delivery will be made to the shipping address mentioned at the time of placing the order. The consignment will not be redirected/redelivered to any other address under any circumstances.

No deliveries will be made on such days as prohibited by the government.

Kemetyl India Markets Pvt. Ltd. delivery agent or partner agent will attempt delivery of the products only once. In case the delivery is not executed during the attempt, the customer shall still be charged for the order and no redelivery attempt will be made.

### Kemetyl India Markets Pvt. Ltd. WILL CONSIDER THE ORDER AS "EXECUTED" IN THE FOLLOWING CASES:

- Delivery not done due to wrong address given by the customers
- Non-availability of the recipients at the address
- Premises locked
- Wrong phone number or phone not working or switched off or not reachable, when tried by the delivery agent of Winged Logistics Services
- Addressee refused to accept the products
- Delivered the product at the security cabin of the building/Reception/Neighbour, based on the instructions from the customer
- In case of civil disturbances, floods, Heavy Rains, National Bandh or in happening any other force majeure event, we reserve the right to cancel the order or reschedule the delivery to another date
- Requests for cancellation of confirmed order can be made up to 24 hours after the order placing delivery time
