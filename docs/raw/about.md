# ABOUT HP Vaahn

## A Powerful Partnership for Premium Automotive Care

At HP Vaahn, we bring together the unmatched legacy of Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) and the global expertise of Kemetyl Group, a leading European brand in automotive fluids and car care solutions. This collaboration marks a new era in high-performance vehicle maintenance, offering world-class products formulated in Europe and packaged in India—delivering superior quality, reliability, and value to Indian consumers.

## About HPCL – A Trusted Name in Energy and Innovation

Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) is one of India's most trusted names in the energy sector. With a strong presence in refining, marketing, and distribution, HPCL has built a reputation for excellence, serving millions of customers through its vast network of fuel stations, LPG services, and industrial products. Known for its commitment to innovation, HPCL consistently introduces cutting-edge solutions that enhance convenience and efficiency for consumers. HPCL's deep-rooted understanding of the Indian market, robust infrastructure, and nationwide reach make it the perfect partner to introduce world-class automotive solutions.

## Why HPCL and Kemetyl Have Come Together

HPCL and Kemetyl share a common vision—to provide world-class automotive care solutions that cater to the evolving needs of vehicle owners in India. This collaboration is driven by:

- **Global Expertise, Local Excellence** – Kemetyl's premium automotive fluids, formulated in Europe, are now made available to Indian consumers through HPCL's trusted network. These products are meticulously tested and refined to meet international and Indian standards, ensuring unparalleled performance.
- **Uncompromising Quality** – Kemetyl's reputation for high-performance products combined with HPCL's rigorous quality control ensures that Indian customers receive top-tier automotive fluids that enhance engine efficiency, safety, and longevity.
- **Made for India, Backed by Global Standards** – While these solutions are based on Kemetyl's European formulations, they are packaged in India to ensure affordability and accessibility, giving consumers the best of both worlds—international quality at local prices.
- **Sustainability and Innovation** – Both HPCL and Kemetyl are committed to sustainability, ensuring that all products are environmentally friendly, minimizing impact while maximizing efficiency.
