## Shipping Policy

**URL:** `/shipping-policy/`

- **Delivery window:** 9:00am–6:00pm on estimated delivery date
- **Delays possible:** Due to extreme weather or governmental regulatory activities
- **Address:** Delivery only to shipping address provided at order — no redirection/redelivery to alternate addresses
- **No deliveries:** On government-prohibited days
- **Single attempt:** Delivery agent attempts delivery only once. If failed, customer still charged and no redelivery

### Order Considered "Executed" When:

1. Wrong address given by customer
2. Recipient unavailable at address
3. Premises locked
4. Wrong phone number / phone switched off / unreachable
5. Addressee refused to accept products
6. Delivered to security cabin / reception / neighbour per customer instructions
7. Force majeure events (civil disturbances, floods, heavy rains, national bandh) — order may be cancelled/rescheduled
8. **Cancellation:** Requests accepted up to 24 hours after placing order
